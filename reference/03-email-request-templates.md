# §58c — Ready-to-Send Email / Request Templates

Fill-in-the-blank request bodies for every source that needs an email, form, or
postal request. Replace every `[BRACKETED]` field with your details before sending.

**Universal fill-ins used throughout:**
`[YOUR ANCESTOR'S NAME]` (with all spelling variants), `[MAIDEN NAME]`,
`[MARRIED NAME]`, `[DOB]`, `[PLACE OF BIRTH]`, `[FLIGHT YEARS]` (e.g. ~1938–39),
`[DESTINATION COUNTRY]`, `[YOUR RELATIONSHIP]` (e.g. grandson/granddaughter),
`[YOUR FULL NAME]`, `[YOUR EMAIL]`, `[YOUR PHONE]`, `[YOUR POSTAL ADDRESS]`,
`[your case number]` (any archival reference you already have).

**Spelling-variant reminder:** always list every plausible surname spelling
(umlaut `ü`, `ue`, and `u` forms) and ask the archive to search all of them, and to
treat **surname + date of birth** as the primary match keys.

## Template 1 — World Jewish Relief (refugee-record tracing)

**Channel:** the "Find Your Family Story" web form at
https://www.worldjewishrelief.org/what-we-do/archives-your-family-history/
(paste the body below into the form's message field; or send as email if a direct
archives address is provided in their reply).

**Subject:** Family record request — [DESTINATION COUNTRY]-refugee from Austria,
[FLIGHT YEARS] ([MAIDEN NAME], b. [DOB] [PLACE OF BIRTH])

**Body:**

Dear World Jewish Relief team,

I'm trying to trace records for my late [ANCESTOR-RELATION — e.g. grandmother], an Austrian
refugee who came to [DESTINATION COUNTRY] around [FLIGHT YEARS] to escape Nazi
persecution. I understand you hold records for children and refugees assisted by the
Refugee Children's Movement, the Central British Fund, and their successors, and I'd
be very grateful for any help.

Her/his details:
- **Name at the time (maiden name):** [MAIDEN NAME] — please search all spellings:
  [VARIANT 1] / [VARIANT 2] / [VARIANT 3].
- **Born:** [DOB], [PLACE OF BIRTH], Austria.
- **Later married name:** [MARRIED NAME] (if applicable).
- **Arrival:** to [DESTINATION COUNTRY] around [FLIGHT YEARS], fleeing Nazi
  persecution; settled there and later naturalised.

One note that may help: she/he would have been about [AGE] in [YEAR], which may be
[within / older than] the typical Kindertransport age. So while the arrival may have
been via a Kindertransport, it may also have been through another refugee or
emigration route — I'd be grateful if you could check for **any refugee, arrival, or
case record around [FLIGHT YEARS], not only Kindertransport records**, so we don't
miss it. (Please search under the **maiden name**, since she/he arrived before
marrying.)

I'm gathering documentation of the flight from Austria to support a citizenship
application. Any record you can locate — or a pointer to where else to look — would
mean a great deal.

With gratitude,
[YOUR FULL NAME]
[YOUR EMAIL] · [YOUR PHONE] · [YOUR POSTAL ADDRESS]

**What to expect back:** an email from the archives team with a scanned registration
card and/or case file (free), or a note that only a card survives. **Attach nothing
up front;** supply proof of descent only if asked.

## Template 2 — IKG Wien (Jewish community vital records)

**Channel:** email **matriken@ikg-wien.at** (Records Office — for a birth/marriage/
death register entry), **CC archiv@ikg-wien.at** (general Archive). Postal fallback:
IKG Wien, Archiv/Matriken, Seitenstettengasse 4, 1010 Vienna, Austria.
**You MUST attach** the completed & signed IKG **user-declaration form** ("Data
Sheet"), a copy of your photo ID, and proof of descent.

**Subject:** Records request + user form attached — [YOUR ANCESTOR'S NAME], b. [DOB]
Vienna (§58c descendant enquiry)

**Body:**

Dear Records Office / Archive team,

I'm writing from [YOUR COUNTRY] to request a copy of a **birth register entry
(Matriken)** for my late [ANCESTOR-RELATION — e.g. grandmother], and any related community or
emigration record you may hold. I've attached your completed and signed **user form
(Data Sheet)**, a copy of my ID, and [PROOF OF DESCENT — e.g. my parent's birth
certificate naming the ancestor] as proof of my direct descent and legal interest.

Details:
- **Name:** [YOUR ANCESTOR'S NAME] — please search all spellings: [VARIANT 1] /
  [VARIANT 2] / [VARIANT 3].
- **Born:** [DOB], Vienna — so the entry is past its 100-year protective period.
- **Later married name:** [MARRIED NAME] (if applicable).
- She/he emigrated from Austria to [DESTINATION COUNTRY] around [FLIGHT YEARS],
  fleeing Nazi persecution, and later naturalised.

Specifically I'd be grateful for:
1. A copy or certified extract of the **[YEAR] Vienna birth register entry** (the
   priority document).
2. Any **emigration/departure record** around [FLIGHT YEARS], or a
   community-membership entry, if your holdings show one.

I understand there is a fee for written research and for issuing certificates, and I
accept those charges — **please send a cost estimate (Kostenvoranschlag) if the
search will be extensive**, and let me know how you'd like payment. This feeds a
declaration under §58c of the Austrian Citizenship Act; if it's simpler to provide the
record directly to the Austrian consulate handling that declaration, I'm glad to
arrange that.

With sincere thanks,
[YOUR FULL NAME]
[YOUR EMAIL] · [YOUR PHONE]

**What to expect / attach:** a cost estimate first for anything extensive; fees
(€50/half-hour research, €12/A4 certificate, €6/A4 scan) are owed even if nothing is
found. **Fee-exemption tip:** if the request is routed through a consulate/ministry it
is fee-exempt — consider letting the consulate or your lawyer pull it instead. English
enquiry accepted; the reply may come in German.

## Template 3 — Wiener Stadt- und Landesarchiv (WStLA) Meldeanfrage

**Channel:** the access-restricted **online form** (English) at
https://www.wien.gv.at/english/e-government/culture/archives/research/residence-registration-records.html
For case-relevant follow-up email **post@archiv.wien.gv.at**. Postal: Wiener Stadt-
und Landesarchiv (MA 8), Guglgasse 14, Gasometer D, 1110 Vienna.

**Subject:** Request for historical residence-registration record (Meldeauskunft) —
[YOUR ANCESTOR'S NAME], b. [DOB]

**Body:**

Dear Wiener Stadt- und Landesarchiv,

I would like to request a historical residence-registration record (historische
Meldeauskunft / Meldezettel) for the following person:

- **Name:** [YOUR ANCESTOR'S NAME]. The surname also appears as [VARIANT 1] /
  [VARIANT 2] / [VARIANT 3], and the given/middle name in different spellings across records.
  Please treat the **surname variants together with the date of birth as the primary
  search keys** — a difference in middle-name spelling should not exclude an otherwise
  matching record.
- **Date of birth:** [DOB]
- **Religion:** [RELIGION, if known]
- **Known history:** She/he lived in Vienna and emigrated to [DESTINATION COUNTRY]
  around [FLIGHT YEARS] to escape Nazi persecution. The deregistration (Abmeldung)
  would therefore date to around that time.

[If you hold an identity-corroborating record about the ancestor, add:] To help
confirm identity I attach [DESCRIBE THE RECORD, e.g. a wartime registration card],
which records the same name, date of birth, and Vienna birthplace.

I would be grateful for a copy of the data from the registration record, specifically:
1. The **last registered Vienna address** before emigration;
2. The **deregistration (Abmeldung) entry**, with date and any recorded destination/
   new address;
3. Any **earlier Vienna addresses** with arrival/departure dates, and the
   **household / co-registered persons** (e.g. parents), if available.

**Relationship and purpose:** [YOUR ANCESTOR'S NAME] was my [RELATIONSHIP]. I am
pursuing Austrian citizenship as a direct descendant under §58c StbG and gathering
documentation of her/his life in Vienna and flight. I can provide proof of descent on
request — please let me know what documentation you require to release this to a
descendant. [For a deceased ancestor you may add: as she/he is deceased, I understand
the deceased-person route may apply.]

I understand a fee of €35 per half-hour of research applies (up to a €140 maximum) and
that the reply will be issued in German; both are fine.

With thanks,
[YOUR FULL NAME]
[YOUR POSTAL ADDRESS]
[YOUR EMAIL] · [YOUR PHONE]

**What to expect back:** a German-language letter within ~2 months stating the data
found (or that none was found — the fee is charged either way). This official letter
is itself a good evidentiary attachment for the declaration.

## Template 4 — Austrian State Archives (ÖStA) enquiry

**Channel:** email **gdpost@oesta.gv.at** (+43 1 795 40 0). First identify the file's
archival signature via findbuch.at (e.g. `AT-OeStA/AdR/E-uReang/VVSt/VA/[file no.]`).

**Subject:** Copy request / cost estimate — file [ARCHIVAL SIGNATURE]
([YOUR ANCESTOR'S NAME])

**Body:**

Dear Austrian State Archives,

I would like to request a copy of, and a cost estimate (Kostenvoranschlag) for, the
following file held in the Archiv der Republik:

- **Person:** [YOUR ANCESTOR'S NAME], born [DOB] [PLACE OF BIRTH]
  [+ profession if known].
- **File / archival signature:** [ARCHIVAL SIGNATURE — e.g.
  AT-OeStA/AdR/E-uReang/VVSt/VA/[file no.]]
- **Record type:** [e.g. 1938 property/asset declaration (Vermögensanmeldung)],
  which I located via findbuch.at.

I am the subject's [YOUR RELATIONSHIP] and am gathering documentation of her/his
persecution to support a declaration under §58c of the Austrian Citizenship Act. Please
let me know the cost and how to proceed, and what proof of descent or identification you
require. I can supply my ID and proof of descent on request.

With thanks,
[YOUR FULL NAME]
[YOUR EMAIL] · [YOUR PHONE] · [YOUR POSTAL ADDRESS]

**What to expect back:** a cost estimate first, then the file copy on payment. The 1938
asset declaration typically carries the ancestor's address, DOB, and profession —
strong primary persecution evidence.

## Template 5 — UK National Archives FOI (open a closed HO 405 file)

**Channel:** on the closed record's page in TNA *Discovery*
(https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk), click **"Submit FOI request"** (it
pre-tags the form to the reference). Alt: the general TNA FOI enquiry form. No cost;
response due in ~20 working days (+10 if TNA consults the Home Office).

**Subject:** FOI request — please open closed record [REFERENCE, e.g. HO 405/xxxxx]
([ANCESTOR NAME], DOB [DOB])

**Body:**

Dear Freedom of Information team,

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2000, I request that the following closed record
be reviewed and opened:

- **Reference:** [REFERENCE — e.g. HO 405/xxxxx] (Discovery ref [DISCOVERY ID];
  former departmental ref [DEPT REF if known])
- **Description:** "[VERBATIM CATALOGUE DESCRIPTION]"
- **Series:** HO 405 — Home Office: Aliens Department, Aliens Personal Files
- **Current status:** closed until [YEAR] under section 40(2), on the ground that it
  "contains sensitive personal information which would substantially distress or
  endanger a living person or his or her descendants."

I believe the basis for closure no longer applies:

1. **The subject was born on [DOB] and would now be [AGE] years old**, and cannot
   reasonably be presumed living. I understand TNA's practice is to open HO 405 files
   once the subject has passed their 100th birthday (or earlier on proof of death).
2. **The subject is deceased.** [State the evidence — e.g. a professional-body or
   alumni directory entry marked "(Deceased)", a death record, or other proof. If you have no
   death record because they may have died abroad, say so and offer to supply one on
   request.]
3. **I am the subject's [YOUR RELATIONSHIP]**, requesting this for family-history
   research. Insofar as the closure rests on possible distress to descendants, I — as a
   descendant — expressly consent to and request disclosure. To establish the
   relationship I attach [PROOF OF DESCENT — e.g. my parent's birth certificate naming
   the subject, and my own].

Should you require documentary proof of death, please let me know; I am pursuing one
and can supply it, but I would be grateful if the evidence above could be considered in
the first instance.

Yours faithfully,
[YOUR FULL NAME]
[YOUR POSTAL ADDRESS]
[YOUR EMAIL]

**Attachments:** proof of descent (parent's birth certificate naming the subject; your
own birth certificate). **What to expect:** a decision within ~20 working days; if
granted, the file (police reports, application, refugee background) — often the deepest
source, and it may contain a spouse's supporting papers.

## Template 6 — GRO certificate order (UK civil registration)

**Channel:** the GRO online ordering service (gov.uk). You need the **District,
Volume, and Page** (or the GRO index reference) — get these free from
https://www.freebmd.org.uk (England & Wales) or Findmypast.

**Order details to enter:**
- **Certificate type:** [birth / marriage / death]
- **Names:** [NAME(S) ON THE CERTIFICATE — for a marriage, both parties]
- **Event date / quarter & year:** [e.g. December quarter, YYYY]
- **Registration district:** [DISTRICT]
- **GRO reference:** Volume [VOL], Page [PAGE]
- **Delivery:** standard (~£12.50, a few working days) or **priority + courier** (higher
  fee, faster) if you need it quickly.

**Note:** for the §58c declaration a **plain (uncertified) copy is fine — no apostille
is needed** on ancestor/descent-chain documents. Certified/apostilled copies of these
are generally only needed later at the passport stage, and only if there was a name
change to prove.

**What to expect back:** the certificate by post (or courier if priority). A marriage
certificate documents a maiden→married surname change; a parent's birth certificate is
a descent-chain link naming the ancestor by maiden name.

## Sending checklist (all templates)

1. Replace **every** `[BRACKETED]` field — do not send with placeholders.
2. List **all** surname spelling variants and ask them to search each.
3. State **surname + DOB as the primary match keys**; note married vs. maiden name and
   which period each applies to.
4. Attach proof of descent only where required (IKG, closed-file FOI,
   access-restricted archives) — otherwise offer it "on request."
5. Verify the current recipient address/form on the institution's site before sending;
   contact points change.
6. Track what you sent and when — turnaround ranges from minutes to ~12 months.
